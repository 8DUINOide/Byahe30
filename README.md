Byahe30 helps you plan when to leave, see the road ahead, and get a reminder when your stop is near. Times are shown in Philippine Time (PHT, UTC+8).

## Plan your trip

1. Choose where you’re starting and where you need to go.
2. Set the time you need to arrive and choose your preferred ride, or select **Any available**.
3. Select **Find my leave time** to see an estimated time to leave home.
4. Review the suggested direct or transfer option. Allow extra time for queues, walking, and changing road conditions.

The estimate includes expected travel time, a typical wait for service, and a last-mile buffer where applicable. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed arrival times or an official transit timetable.

## Track your ride

While travelling, choose your direction and drop-off stop under **Track My Ride · Para Alert**. Check the landmark notes beneath the stop selector, then select **Just Track Me**.

If you allow location access, Byahe30 can show the nearest checkpoint and estimate the remaining time. Para Alert can notify you when you are within 500 metres of your selected stop. Keep the page open and your device’s sound or vibration available; browser and device settings may affect alerts.

Location access is optional. Your GPS location stays in your browser and is not sent to or stored by Byahe30.

## Reading the map and estimates

- The map follows the Baao–Pili–Naga road corridor. Stop positions are approximate landmarks, not verified boarding points.
- **Estimated waves** are timetable-based projections, not live vehicle locations. Their time window is an estimate.
- Check the corridor status label. **Baseline only** means the display is using historical or planning estimates rather than a live traffic reading.
- Traffic, weather, waiting times, service availability, and GPS accuracy can change. Confirm local conditions before travelling.

Byahe30 is an independent planning aid, not an official service of a transport provider or government agency. It does not show confirmed departures or guarantee that a ride is available.
